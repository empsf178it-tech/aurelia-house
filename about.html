OUR ETHOS

MORE THAN A RESTAURANT.

A gathering place built on genuine warmth, unhurried time, and purposeful cooking.

THE BEGINNING

OUR STORY

AURELIA was founded in 2021 as a quiet antidote to rushed dining culture.

Co-founders Chef Julian Vance and creative director Clara Moreau envisioned a dining space that felt like an extended dining room of a close friend—warm, refined, filled with natural textures, ambient candlelight, and food cooked with absolute reverence.

Named after the golden light of late afternoon (“Aurelia”), the restaurant honors the sacred hours when work yields to rest, conversation, and shared meals.

THREE GUIDING PILLARS

OUR PHILOSOPHY

01

GOOD INGREDIENTS

We never mask bad produce with heavy technique. Superior flavor begins in healthy soil and clean waters.

02

THOUGHTFUL COOKING

Restraint over complexity. Open-fire cooking, slow reductions, and techniques that honor each item's natural profile.

03

WARM HOSPITALITY

Elegance without coldness. Genuine attentiveness that respects your space and creates a sense of belonging.

THE ATMOSPHERE

OUR TABLE & APPROACH

A place where people gather. Simple. Seasonal. Intentional.

Whether you come for a quick weekday dinner or an extended multi-course tasting evening, our tables are designed with comfortable acoustic proportions, warm tactile walnut, soft linen, and muted lighting so you can focus entirely on the food and each other.
